Terms of Service

These terms govern use of NIMBLE Race Control within NIMBLE Racing Team's Discord community.

1. What the bot does

NIMBLE Race Control supports driver registration, verified racing results, career XP, driver classification roles, race announcements, and leaderboards for community-hosted Car Parking Multiplayer 2 (CPM2) events.

2. Participation and fair play

  • Race participation is voluntary and subject to NIMBLE's published event rules and Discord's applicable rules.
  • Only authorized officials may enter or approve event results or change racing XP.
  • Participants must not impersonate other drivers, falsify results, or attempt to exploit the points system.
  • Officials may correct verified errors in standings and XP, with an audit record.

3. Ranks and awards

XP, ranks, and standings are virtual community achievements. They have no cash value and do not grant real-world racing licences, FIA accreditation, employment, or prizes unless separately and explicitly announced under lawful event rules.

4. Availability and changes

This is a community-operated bot. Features may be changed, paused, or removed for maintenance, security, or fair-play reasons. Results may be corrected when errors are identified. No uninterrupted operation is guaranteed.

5. Responsible conduct

Use of the bot must comply with Discord's terms and community standards and NIMBLE's server rules. Misuse may result in restriction of bot features or participation in events.

6. Disclaimer and affiliation

NIMBLE Race Control is a community project and is not endorsed, administered, or certified by Discord, the FIA, or CPM2's developers. It does not facilitate real-money vehicle trading.

7. Contact

For questions about these terms, contact the NIMBLE Racing Team Team Owner or TeamManager through the community Discord server.
