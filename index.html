Welcome to NIMBLE Race Control

A community Discord bot for NIMBLE Racing Team's Car Parking Multiplayer 2 competitions.

Race Control manages voluntary driver registration, race results, career experience points, classification roles, and leaderboards for events approved by NIMBLE staff.
